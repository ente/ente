# Uncategorized

**Uncategorized** is a special album where photos are automatically added under the following circumstances:

- When you remove a photo from its last album, it moves to Uncategorized
- During album deletion, if you choose to keep photos but delete the album, all photos exclusive to that album move to Uncategorized

> [!NOTE]
>
> This does not include photos uploaded by others.

## Organize Uncategorized photos {#organize-uncategorized-photos}

**On desktop:**

1. Open Uncategorized and select the photos you want to organize
2. Choose **Move** in the action bar and select a destination
3. To place the same photos in more albums, open the first destination album, select them, and choose **Add to album**
4. Repeat for each additional album; the desktop picker uses one destination at a time

**On mobile:** Select the photos and use **Add to album** to choose one or more destination albums.

Use these album actions for photos already in Ente. **Upload** selects new files from your computer. A photo can belong to multiple Ente albums while counting only once toward your storage.

## Cleaning up Uncategorized items

![Uncategorized album with photos on Ente Photos mobile and web](https://docs-assets.ente.com/screenshots/duo-uncategorized-view.png){width=900px}

**On mobile:**

1. Open the Uncategorized album
2. Tap the overflow menu (three dots)
3. Select **Clean Uncategorized**

![Clean Uncategorized option highlighted in the overflow menu on Ente Photos mobile](https://docs-assets.ente.com/screenshots/phone-uncategorized-clean-callout.png){width=320px}

**On web/desktop:**

1. Open the Uncategorized album
2. Click the clean icon (broom)

![Clean Uncategorized control highlighted on Ente Photos web](https://docs-assets.ente.com/screenshots/web-uncategorized-clean-callout.png){width=720px}

All files that are also present in another album you own will be removed from Uncategorized.
