# Collect wedding and event photos

Make one private album. Ente lets guests upload from a browser link, with no app or account needed.

## Set it up in Ente

1. Create an album in the [Ente app](https://ente.com/app), for example "Emma & James, 12 Dec".
2. Share the album with a link, then turn on **Allow adding photos** in the link settings. Ente calls this a collect link. Learn more in [Public links](/photos/features/sharing-and-collaboration/public-links).
3. Set a password, an expiry date, and a device limit. An expiry about a month after the day works well.

    The link stays end-to-end encrypted, and the key sits in the part of the link after the `#`, which never reaches Ente's servers, so anyone with the link can open the album. That's why the password matters ([Sharing and collaboration FAQ](/photos/faq/sharing-and-collaboration#public-link-encryption)).

    On the free plan, a public link works on up to 10 devices. A paid plan can create public links with no device limit. A link created on the free plan keeps that limit until you set **Device limit** to **None**. For a big wedding, use a paid plan. Learn more in the [Sharing and collaboration FAQ](/photos/faq/sharing-and-collaboration#keep-public-link-after-device-limit). Plans are in [Sign up](/photos/getting-started/signup).

4. Make a QR code for the tables and the invitation. The Ente mobile app can share a public link as a QR code (added in v1.2.11, [changelog](/photos/changelog)). Look for it when you share the album's link.
5. Optional: use your own domain, such as `photos.yourwedding.com`. Custom domains for links are available to everyone, including the free plan. Learn more in [Custom domains](/photos/features/sharing-and-collaboration/custom-domains/).

## What guests see

They open the link in a browser, enter the password if you set one, and can view the album and add photos from their phone. You won't see which guest added which photo. Uploads from people without an Ente account appear without attribution ([Sharing and collaboration FAQ](/photos/faq/sharing-and-collaboration#collect-link-attribution)).

## On the day

Set this up before the day. Put the link or a QR code on the tables.

## After the wedding

- Turn off uploads, or let the link expire.
- Download everything to your computer with the desktop app's export. Learn more in [Export](/photos/migration/export/).
- Share a view-only link with family for the final album.

## Other options

**iCloud Shared Albums.** Easy for iPhone guests. Starting in iOS 27, guests can add photos on the web without an Apple Account, and Apple says Shared Albums add full-resolution sharing. On iOS 26 and earlier, Apple says shared albums upload a copy, and downloaded content may not contain the same information as the original ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108314)). Ente's [Import from Apple Photos](/photos/migration/from-apple-photos/) guide describes those copies as reduced quality, with location data removed. Shared Albums aren't end-to-end encrypted, even with Advanced Data Protection on ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108756)).

**Google Photos.** Shared albums work on any phone. Google lets you share with people even if they don't use the Google Photos app ([Google](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6131416)).

**Wedding upload sites.** Some services are made just for this. Check how long they keep the photos, and whether you can download everything at full quality.

## FAQ

### Do guests need to install Ente? {#collect-event-no-app}

No. They upload from the browser ([Sharing and collaboration FAQ](/photos/faq/sharing-and-collaboration#collect-without-account)).

### Can I collect photos for other events? {#collect-event-other-events}

Yes. Birthdays, school trips, and reunions work the same way.
