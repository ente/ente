# Library sharing

Library sharing lets you share the albums you own with a family member in one action. You can also automatically share new albums as you create them.

Library sharing is available in Ente Photos on iOS and Android. The recipient must be an active member of your [family plan](/photos/features/account/family-plans).

## Share albums

1. Open `Settings > Family`.
2. Select a family member and tap **Share albums**.
3. Select the albums to share, or tap **Select all**.
4. Choose Viewer, Collaborator, or Admin for the selection. You can also assign roles per album.
5. Tap the **Share** button.

The albums appear among the recipient's shared albums. New photos added to them remain available to the recipient as they are backed up.

See [Collaboration](/photos/features/sharing-and-collaboration/collaboration#permissions-explained) for the permissions granted by each role.

## Share new albums automatically

Open the same **Share albums** page and enable **Library sharing**. Choose the default role to use for albums shared automatically.

Ente shares your current albums and automatically shares new albums you create. It skips:

- Hidden albums
- Albums you stopped sharing or that the family member left
- Albums shared with you by someone else

You can select a previously unshared album manually. Unhide a hidden album before sharing it.

## Manage library sharing

On a family member's **Share albums** page, you can:

- Share more albums
- Change the roles assigned to shared albums
- Stop sharing selected albums
- Disable automatic library sharing

Disabling library sharing does not revoke existing shares or stop new photos from appearing in those albums. It only stops new albums from being shared automatically.

If the family member leaves your family plan, automatic sharing with them is disabled. Existing album shares remain until you stop sharing them.

## Privacy

Library sharing uses the same end-to-end encryption as other shared albums. Your device encrypts each album key for the recipient before sending it to Ente. Ente's servers receive the account and album identifiers, roles, and sharing source required to establish and enforce shares, but cannot decrypt album keys, photos, album names, or comments and likes.

Library sharing preferences are also end-to-end encrypted and synced across your devices.

## Platform support

You can configure library sharing on iOS and Android. Albums shared through it are regular shared albums and remain accessible through Ente's mobile, web, and desktop apps. Library sharing controls are coming to web and desktop later.
