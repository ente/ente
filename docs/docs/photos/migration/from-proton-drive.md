# Import from Proton Drive

Download your photos from Proton Drive on the web, then drag the folders into the Ente desktop app. For a large library, download in batches and check your counts before deleting anything.

## What you need

- A computer with enough free space for your whole library.
- Your Proton account password, and a two-factor code if you use one.
- Time. A large library can take several sessions.

## Step 1: Find your photos

Your photos may be in more than one place: photos backed up from the Proton Drive phone app, and folders you uploaded yourself. Check both.

## Step 2: Download from the web app

1. Go to [drive.proton.me](https://drive.proton.me) and sign in.
2. Open the folder you want.
3. Select the photos or folders. You can select several at a time.
4. Click **Download**. When you download several items at once, Proton saves them as a single ZIP ([Proton](https://proton.me/support/set-up-proton-drive)).
5. Unzip it into a folder on your computer, then download the next batch.

If a large ZIP fails, select fewer items. Proton's [Drive help](https://proton.me/support/drive) has the current details.

Proton has desktop apps for Windows and macOS ([Proton Drive download](https://proton.me/drive/download)), but its [Photos help](https://proton.me/support/drive/photos) doesn't describe a full photo export from them. The web app is the reliable route.

## Step 3: Check dates and locations

Open a few photos and check that their dates and locations look right. Then compare the number of files on your computer with the number Proton shows.

## Step 4: Upload to Ente

In the Ente desktop app, sign in and drag the folder in. To keep each subfolder as its own album, choose **Separate albums**. Learn more in [Import from local hard disk](/photos/migration/from-local-hard-disk).

Keep your Proton copy until you've checked everything arrived in Ente.

## Why move from Proton Drive to Ente?

Proton Drive and Ente are both end-to-end encrypted, so privacy isn't the difference. The difference is what each is built for. Proton Drive is a cloud drive for all your files. Ente is a photos app, with face grouping, search, a map, and shared albums for family.

Face grouping, search, and the map run on your device, so Ente never sees your photos ([Security and privacy FAQ](/photos/faq/security-and-privacy#ml-privacy)). Shared albums are end-to-end encrypted too ([Sharing](/photos/features/sharing-and-collaboration/share)).

[Get the Ente desktop app](https://ente.com/download/) and drag your downloaded folders in.
